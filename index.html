What I work on

Earth observation and remote sensing

Flood mapping, building detection, and riverbank erosion from satellite imagery. Much of this work asks what happens when a sensor is unavailable or the resolution drops — the conditions under which these systems are actually deployed.

Optical–SAR fusion · Self-supervised segmentation · Masked autoencoders

Efficient and trustworthy models

Knowledge distillation, reinforcement learning for optimisation, and explainable AI. The aim is models that are small enough to deploy and transparent enough that someone can check why they made a decision.

Knowledge distillation · Reinforcement learning · SHAP and LIME

Machine learning for health and biology

Diagnosis from medical images with limited labels, federated training that keeps patient data inside the hospital, and graph-based methods for protein structure and drug discovery.

Foundation model adaptation · Federated learning · Graph neural networks

Work in progress

Tuberculosis screening without sharing patient data

A federated learning setup that trains a chest X-ray classifier across several hospitals while each hospital's images never leave its own servers.

TensorFlow · Federated learning · Python

Neurosymbolic models that can explain themselves

Pairing neural networks with symbolic reasoning so a clinical prediction comes with a chain of reasoning a doctor can inspect rather than a bare score.

Neural networks · Symbolic reasoning · Explainability

Protein folds for faster drug discovery

Attention-based graph neural networks with reinforcement learning, searching for low-energy folds in viral protein RNA to narrow down drug candidates sooner.

Graph neural networks · Reinforcement learning · Computational biology
